Before You Book

Quick answers

How fast is setting up?

One venue takes minutes to configure and automations are instant. Our onboarding team gets your first offers out the same day.

What support do new teams get?

Hands-on onboarding, priority support and a direct product channel — we configure venues, templates and email flows with you.

How are payouts and taxes handled?

Deposits, guarantees and artist payouts are recorded and reconciled in-platform. Payouts processed through Hedlyner come with accounting-ready records and 1099 support.

Do artists need accounts?

No — artists can reply to invites and offers without signing up. Accounts are optional unless they're paid through Hedlyner, and we'll walk you through that.
