Workflow Overview

How Hedlyner makes booking work

Import, automate, book and pay — one workflow that keeps every show on track.

01 — IMPORT YOUR CONTACTS

Bring everyone into one directory

Upload spreadsheets to bring artists, agents and venues into one clean, searchable directory. We match and normalize names, emails and acts so every past show, note and rider follows the person.

02 — SET UP YOUR AUTOMATIONS

Configure once, reuse everywhere

Create venue defaults, offer templates and notification flows once — then reuse them. Stages, tech specs, terms and reminders auto-fill every offer so your shows are accurate and consistent.

03 — CREATE A BOOKING

From date to confirmed, in one thread

With venue defaults set, add date, time and booking details, pick who to invite, and send offers. Responses attach to the booking thread and statuses (Hold → Offered → Confirmed) keep the whole team aligned.

04 — SET UP PAYMENTS

Money stays tied to the booking

Collect deposits and guarantees, schedule artist payouts and keep every payment tied to its booking. Export accounting-ready reports — and when payments are processed through Hedlyner, we handle 1099 compliance for you.

Quick Answers

A few common questions

What teams usually ask before their demo.

How fast is setting up?

Set up is fast. One venue takes minutes to configure and automations are instant. Our onboarding team is there to make setup run smoothly and get your first offers out the same day.

What support do new teams get?

Hands-on onboarding, priority support and a direct product channel. We work with your team to configure venues, templates and email flows, and iterate based on your feedback.

How are payouts and tax obligations handled?

Payments are tied to bookings: deposits, guarantees and artist payouts are recorded and reconciled in-platform. When payouts are processed through Hedlyner, we provide accounting-ready records and support 1099 compliance for qualifying payments.

Do artists also need to sign up?

Artists do not need accounts — they can reply to invites and offers without signing up, and their replies attach to the booking thread. Artist accounts are optional if you want them to manage profiles or access extra features. If payments are processed through Hedlyner, artists will need an account to add their financial details; we'll walk you through how we meet financial and regulatory requirements with our payments partner.
