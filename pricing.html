Good to know

The fine print, unfineprinted.

Payment processing

Credit card payments: 3.5% pass-through. ACH transfers: 1% pass-through. These are payment network fees — not Hedlyner revenue.

Talent placement commission

When Hedlyner acts as booking agent and sources a placement for you, a standard 8% commission is deducted from the talent's payout — consistent with industry-standard booking agent rates (10–20%). No additional charge to the venue.
