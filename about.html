Why we exist

The music was always there. The infrastructure to support it never was.

Across cities, the same thing was happening. Venues were closing — turning into condos, restaurants, anything that wasn't a room with a stage. Rents climbed. Artists moved. The texture of neighborhood life got quieter. It wasn't just a real estate story. It was a cultural one. The independent musicians who make a city feel like itself — the raw ones, the ones who play because they have to — were being priced out of the places that needed them most.

And here was the strange part: the demand was never the problem. Small businesses, bars, restaurants, community spaces — they wanted live music. Artists wanted the gigs. The will existed on both sides. What didn't exist was a way to actually make it happen without the process eating you alive. Red tape. Endless back-and-forth. No agreements, no payment structure, no follow-through. People would get halfway there and give up.

Then the pandemic came. Then it lifted. And when it did, audiences came back with something to prove — a hunger for real, physical, human experience that no algorithm could replicate. The renaissance was real. The appetite was there. And independent live music was exactly what people were reaching for. But the operational layer still hadn't caught up.

Hedlyner was built to close that gap. Not for the headliners. For the independent artists who deserve a real career. For the venues that know live music drives foot traffic, increases sales, and builds community — and want to do it right. For every booking that should have happened but didn't because the process was the problem.

The mission
Make live music booking as reliable and operationally simple as any other part of running a venue — so the music can actually happen.
Where we're headed

Mission & Vision

Mission

Remove the operational friction between great venues and great artists.

Every hour a talent buyer spends chasing a W-9 or reconciling a payment is an hour not spent programming better music. Every week an artist waits to get paid is a week of financial uncertainty that shouldn't exist. Hedlyner exists to eliminate both — so the business of live music can finally match the quality of the music itself.

Vision

The operating system for live music — connecting every side of the market.

A world where any venue can discover vetted talent in minutes, any artist can surface to the right buyers in their market, and any agency can manage hundreds of rooms from a single platform. Not a marketplace. An infrastructure layer — the one the live music industry never had but always needed.

What we stand for

Our principles

Six non-negotiables that shape every decision — from the product roadmap to how we talk to a venue on a Tuesday afternoon.

Industry-first, always

We come from live music. Every feature is built by people who've run venues, toured stages, or negotiated contracts — not just engineers who've read about it.

Speed over bureaucracy

A booking should take minutes, not days. We obsess over removing every unnecessary step — from offer to deposit to show night — so venues and artists can focus on the music.

Fair for both sides

We don't play favorites. Hedlyner is built to give venues the tools to scale and artists the visibility to grow — because a healthy ecosystem requires both to win.

Radical transparency

No hidden fees. No surprise deductions. Artists see exactly what they'll earn. Venues see exactly what they'll pay. Every transaction is documented and traceable.

Operational reliability

Live music runs on trust. Venues trust artists to show up. Artists trust venues to pay on time. Hedlyner is the infrastructure that makes both sides of that trust automatic — not something anyone has to chase.

Data-driven decisions

Gut feelings are great on stage. Off stage, you need numbers. Hedlyner surfaces the data talent buyers need to book smarter — which artists perform, which nights sell, which venues scale.
