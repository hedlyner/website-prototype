HEDLYNER PRIVACY POLICY

Effective: January 1, 2026

1. Our commitment to your privacy

Our Privacy Policy tells you what personally identifiable information Hedlyner, Inc. ("Hedlyner," "we," "us") may collect from you, how Hedlyner may process your personally identifiable information, how you can limit Hedlyner's use of your personally identifiable information, and your rights to obtain, modify and/or delete any personally identifiable information we have collected from you. This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through Hedlyner's website, mobile apps and Platform (collectively, the "Platform").

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide Hedlyner

You may provide information directly to Hedlyner when you register for an account, create or update your profile (including an artist page or venue profile), create or manage Bookings, Contacts, contact support, or otherwise communicate with us. This includes:

  • Account & profile information: name, username, email address, phone number, billing address, profile pictures, artist/venue descriptions, contact details and other identifying details.
  • Uploaded content: images, media, promotional materials, documents, attachments and other content you upload to the Platform.
  • Communications: messages you send through the Platform or to our support or legal teams, including any information you include in those messages.
  • Verification & KYC information: government ID or other identity documentation if required for payments, payouts, or compliance.
  • Third-party contact data that you upload (see section 2.4 below).

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you use the Platform we automatically collect some information about your device, browser, and usage, including:

  • Device and technical data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, operating system, screen resolution and language preferences.
  • Usage and performance data: pages viewed, features used, timestamps, errors and performance metrics.
  • Log data & diagnostics: server logs, crash reports and related technical data.

We use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to collect this information. See Section 2.3 for details.

2.3 Cookies

Hedlyner may use cookies, web beacons, local storage, and similar technologies to provide, protect and improve the Platform and your experience. Cookies help us remember your preferences, support security, provide analytics, and enable certain features. We may also use cookies and similar technologies for advertising and tracking where we have your consent or a lawful basis. You can manage cookie settings at the browser level or via any consent tool we provide; blocking cookies may reduce functionality.

2.4 Third-party contacts uploaded by users

A core Platform feature is the ability for Hedlyner users (for example, Bookers, Venues or Artists) to upload or import contact lists or individual contact records for people who are not Hedlyner users ("Third-Party Contacts"). This may include:

  • Name, email, phone number, affiliation/role, notes and other information provided by the uploading user.
  • Records of communications Hedlyner sends to those contacts on behalf of the uploading user (delivery status, opens, clicks, unsubscribes, etc.).

Important representation by users: When a user uploads or imports Third-Party Contacts, the uploading user represents that they have a lawful basis to provide those contacts to Hedlyner and to permit Hedlyner to process and contact those persons for the purposes described in this Policy (including sending transactional or marketing messages as instructed). See Section 5 (Users' obligations) for more details.

2.5 Payment & transaction data

We collect payment-related information in connection with Bookings and Platform transactions. Hedlyner uses Stripe to process payments and manage payouts. Hedlyner stores only limited identifiers and metadata necessary to reconcile and manage payments (for example, Stripe customer IDs, PaymentIntent IDs, connected account IDs and transaction metadata). Hedlyner does not store full credit card or bank account numbers — those are tokenized and held by Stripe.

3. How we use information

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate the Platform, including account management, Booking creation and execution, payment processing, and sending transactional communications (Booking confirmations, receipts, reminders).
  • To facilitate payments and payouts, including commission calculation, refunds, and tax reporting.
  • To send communications on behalf of users (transactional messages and, where authorized, marketing messages) and to collect delivery and engagement metadata for deliverability and troubleshooting.
  • To improve the Platform, including product analytics, aggregated reporting, model training and personalization based on booking history and usage data ("learning from data").
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse or wrongdoing, to comply with law, and to protect our rights.
  • To comply with legal obligations (tax reporting, lawful requests from authorities), and to enforce our Terms and Policies.

We will only process personal data where we have a lawful basis (contract performance, legitimate interest, consent, or legal obligation) and, where required by law, we will obtain consent.

4. Legal bases for processing (EEA / UK)

If you are located in the EEA or the UK, our legal bases for processing include:

  • Contractual necessity: to perform the contract between you and Hedlyner (for example, to operate bookings or process payments).
  • Legitimate interests: for platform security, analytics, product improvement, and to ensure deliverability and fraud prevention — subject to a balancing test.
  • Consent: where required (for example, non-essential cookies or direct marketing where law requires).
  • Legal obligation: to comply with statutory duties and court orders.

5. Users' obligations when uploading Third-Party Contacts

If you are a Hedlyner user and you upload or import Third-Party Contacts, you must:

  1. Have a lawful basis to provide those contacts to Hedlyner and permit Hedlyner to contact them on your behalf (consent, contractual necessity, or other lawful basis as required by law).
  2. Comply with applicable privacy and anti-spam laws when instructing Hedlyner to contact those persons.
  3. Honor opt-outs, suppression lists and abuse reports for contacts you upload.
  4. Use the contact import tools responsibly (no unlawful bulk scraping or purchase of data with unclear provenance).

If you do not have a lawful basis to upload or contact an individual, do not upload that person's information. Hedlyner's Terms of Service require users to indemnify Hedlyner against claims arising from improper uploads or communications. Hedlyner may suspend or terminate access to upload features for abuse or violations.

6. Email & messaging: transactional vs. marketing, and tracking

6.1 Transactional messages

Hedlyner and our users send transactional messages (Booking confirmations, changes, reminders, payment receipts) that are necessary to operate Bookings. Transactional messages are sent on behalf of users and are treated as required communications.

6.2 Marketing messages

Marketing or promotional messages are sent only with an appropriate lawful basis (consent where required) and include an easy opt-out mechanism. If you are a recipient of marketing messages and wish to unsubscribe, use the unsubscribe link or contact support@hedlyner.com.

6.3 Tracking & legitimate interest balancing test

When transactional messages are sent, Hedlyner collects delivery and engagement metadata (delivery status, opens, clicks, unsubscribes, bounce status) for the limited purposes of:

  • Improving deliverability and ensuring messages are received;
  • Detecting and preventing abuse, fraud and spam;
  • Operating and troubleshooting the Platform.

For transactional message tracking we rely on contract performance and legitimate interest, having undertaken a balancing test which concluded that: (a) the processing is narrowly tailored to operational needs and security; (b) it is necessary to provide reliable communications on the Platform; and (c) it does not unduly infringe on recipients' privacy because only metadata is collected and retention is limited. For marketing tracking, we obtain consent where legally required.

Providers used for messaging & tracking: Twilio, SendGrid (Twilio SendGrid), and Zoho are used to send and track messages. Hedlyner works with these providers and they may collect additional metadata as described in their policies.

7. Payments, fees & Stripe

  • Stripe & tokenization. Hedlyner uses Stripe (including Stripe Connect) to process payments and manage payouts. Stripe tokenizes card and bank details. Hedlyner stores only the identifiers and metadata needed to manage transactions (for example, Stripe customer IDs, payment intent IDs, and connected account IDs). Hedlyner does not store full payment card numbers or bank account numbers.
  • Marketplace model & commissions. Hedlyner operates a marketplace model: payments for Bookings are processed via Stripe and Platform fees/commissions are applied per the Payment Terms. Depending on the transaction and the connected account type, Stripe may act as the payment processor and/or merchant of record. We will clearly disclose at the time of payment who is the charging party and the applicable fees.
  • Payouts & holds. Payout timing and any holds are subject to Stripe's payout schedules and verification requirements. Hedlyner may delay or hold payouts to address disputes, suspected fraud, or compliance matters.
  • Refunds & disputes. Refunds are processed following the Refund & Cancellation Policy; chargebacks and disputes are handled through Stripe's dispute processes and Hedlyner may submit evidence on behalf of users.

8. Who we share your information with & subprocessors

8.1 Categories of recipients

We share personal information with:

  • Service providers/customers' subprocessors that perform services on our behalf (hosting, analytics, payments, messaging).
  • Users and parties to a Booking as required to perform the booked services.
  • Legal & regulatory authorities when required by law or court order.
  • Affiliates and business partners where you consent or it is reasonably necessary to provide services.

8.2 Named subprocessors

We maintain a public list of subprocessors. The primary subprocessors include:

  • Stripe — payments and payouts (Stripe Connect).
  • SendGrid (Twilio SendGrid) — transactional and marketing email delivery and tracking.
  • Twilio — SMS and communications.
  • Zoho — CRM and marketing automation.
  • Google — analytics, email services, and infrastructure.

We require subprocessors to enter into appropriate contractual terms that meet our privacy and security requirements. We will provide prior notice of material changes to subprocessors as required by law or contract.

9. International transfers

We and our subprocessors process and store data in the United States and other countries. Where personal data is transferred from the EEA/UK to jurisdictions that do not provide an adequate level of protection, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), adequacy decisions, or other lawful mechanisms. Contact legal@hedlyner.com for details about transfer mechanisms.

10. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described and to meet legal or business needs. Typical retention periods:

  • Payment & tax records (payments, invoices): 7 years.
  • Booking records & contract information: for the life of the account and for 7 years after the last booking or account closure.
  • Imported Third-Party Contacts: retained until deleted by the uploading user, or for 24 months following last activity if not deleted (unless a longer retention period is required by law).
  • Email & message engagement metadata (opens/clicks): 24 months.
  • Analytics & ML training data (identifiable): 3 years; aggregated or de-identified data may be retained indefinitely.
  • Logs, backups & security records: retention may vary; up to 7 years where necessary for compliance or investigations.

If law requires a longer retention period, we will retain data as necessary to comply.

11. Controlling your information — rights & how to exercise them

11.1 Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate information;
  • Request deletion of personal data (subject to exceptions);
  • Request restriction of or object to certain processing;
  • Request portability of data you have provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format;
  • Withdraw consent where we relied on consent; and
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Hedlyner does not sell or share personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising as of the effective date of this Policy.

11.2 How to make a request

To exercise your rights, email legal@hedlyner.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject and tell us the right you wish to exercise. We will verify your identity before responding and will normally respond within 30 days. Complex requests may take up to 45 days; we will notify you if we require more time.

11.3 Special rules for Third-Party Contacts

If you are a Third-Party Contact whose information was uploaded by a Hedlyner user, we will cooperate with you to exercise rights. To the extent Hedlyner is processing the data as a processor on behalf of the uploading user (controller), we will work with that controller to address the request. We will also accept direct requests to legal@hedlyner.com and will handle them in accordance with applicable law and our contract obligations.

12. Security & incident response

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, access controls, logging, vulnerability management and administrative safeguards. We require similar protections of our subprocessors and conduct vendor security assessments.

Breach notification. If we become aware of a data breach affecting personal data, we will investigate promptly and, if required by law, notify affected individuals and regulators. For incidents requiring notification under GDPR, we aim to notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours where feasible.

We are continuing to enhance our controls around Third-Party Contact uploads (for example, rolling out rate limits, suppression lists and abuse reporting tools) and will update this Policy as those protections are implemented.

13. Cookies & tracking (expanded)

We use cookies and tracking technologies for essential functionality, analytics, performance, and marketing. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser or through any preference center we provide. For EU/UK visitors, non-essential cookies and tracking will be deployed only where required by law (consent).

14. Children's privacy

Our Platform is not intended for children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 16, please contact legal@hedlyner.com and we will take steps to remove the information.

If we make material changes that reduce your rights or increase your obligations, we will post a conspicuous notice and, where appropriate, provide direct notice (for example by email).
