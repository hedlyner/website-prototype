§ 01 — Why we exist

Live music is back at record levels. The booking workflow is still stuck in 2005.

01

Independent venues are selling out nights, growing their lineups, and expanding into new rooms — but the operational layer has not kept pace. Talent buyers are managing dozens of artists across spreadsheets, text threads, and email chains. Artists are waiting weeks for payment. Agencies running ten venues are running ten separate processes held together with copy-paste.

02

Hedlyner was built by people who know what that 2 a.m. spreadsheet looks like from the inside. Our founders came from venue operations and live music management — not Silicon Valley. Every workflow in the platform was designed around how bookings actually happen, not how someone imagined they might.

03

The mission: make live music booking as reliable and operationally simple as any other part of running a venue — as easy as ordering supplies.

The spreadsheet era is over. Live music deserves better infrastructure.

§ 02 — Where we're headed

Mission & Vision

Mission · M/01
M.

Remove the operational friction between great venues and great artists.

Every hour a talent buyer spends chasing a W-9 or reconciling a payment is an hour not spent programming better music. Every week an artist waits to get paid is a week of financial uncertainty that shouldn't exist. Hedlyner exists to eliminate both — so the business of live music can finally match the quality of the music itself.

Vision · V/01
V.

The operating system for live music — connecting every side of the market.

A world where any venue can discover vetted talent in minutes, any artist can surface to the right buyers in their market, and any agency can manage hundreds of rooms from a single platform. Not a marketplace. An infrastructure layer — the one the live music industry never had but always needed.

§ 03 — The team

Built from the inside out

Hector Corral CEO

The person who knows where the live music industry is broken — and exactly how to fix it.

01

Hector Corral spent years inside the operational reality of live music before building Hedlyner. He has worked on both sides of the booking relationship — understanding firsthand how venues make programming decisions and how artists navigate the path from discovery to payment. That dual vantage point is not a coincidence. It is the product.

02

When Hector looked at the live music booking market, he saw an industry in a specific and solvable kind of pain: not a discovery problem, but an operations problem. The same venues that could sell out a Friday night were losing hours each week to manual workflows that no tool had been built to handle — because no tool had been built by someone who understood them at this level. Hedlyner was the answer he built for himself first.

03

The platform's core design decisions — offer flow, integrated 1099 compliance, same-week payouts, multi-venue agency tooling — reflect choices made by someone who has sat in both the venue director's chair and the artist's green room. That is the unfair advantage Hedlyner carries into every competitive conversation.

§ 04 — What we stand for

Our principles

Six non-negotiables that shape every decision we make — from the product roadmap to how we talk to a venue on a Tuesday afternoon.

P / 01

Industry-first, always

We come from live music. Every feature is built by people who've run venues, toured stages, or negotiated contracts — not just engineers who've read about it.

P / 02

Speed over bureaucracy

A booking should take minutes, not days. We obsess over removing every unnecessary step — from offer to deposit to show night — so venues and artists can focus on the music.

P / 03

Fair for both sides

We don't play favorites. Hedlyner is built to give venues the tools to scale and artists the visibility to grow — because a healthy ecosystem requires both to win.

P / 04

Radical transparency

No hidden fees. No surprise deductions. Artists see exactly what they'll earn. Venues see exactly what they'll pay. Every transaction is documented and traceable.

P / 05

Operational reliability

Live music runs on trust. Venues trust artists to show up. Artists trust venues to pay on time. Hedlyner is the infrastructure that makes both sides of that trust automatic — not something anyone has to chase.

P / 06

Data-driven decisions

Gut feelings are great on stage. Off stage, you need numbers. Hedlyner surfaces the data talent buyers need to book smarter — which artists perform, which nights sell, which venues scale.
