Why we exist

San Francisco, 2021. The venues were empty. The artists were ready.

When live music started coming back after the pandemic, something unexpected happened: the booking infrastructure had collapsed. Experienced talent buyers had moved on. Venues had no easy way to find and vet artists. Artists had no reliable way to get in front of decision-makers.

Our founders — a musician and a venue operator — had lived this problem from both sides. They set out to build a single platform where venues could discover vetted local talent, manage bookings end-to-end, and pay artists on time, every time.

The mission hasn't changed: help small businesses grow customer loyalty by regularly booking live music — and make it as easy as ordering supplies.

The spreadsheet era is over. Live music deserves better infrastructure.
Hector Corral, Co-founder & CEO
Our Journey

From idea to industry platform

2021
Founded in San Francisco
Hedlyner is born out of frustration — a musician and a venue operator team up to rebuild the live music booking stack from scratch. First customers: three SF venues and a handful of local jazz artists.
2022
MVP: Booking meets payments
First version ships with artist discovery, offer flow, and integrated payments. Venues stop chasing artists for W-9s. Artists start getting paid in days, not months. Word spreads fast.
2023
Platform evolution — V2
Multi-venue management, calendar intelligence, and CRM-grade booking history land in V2. Talent buying agencies with 10+ venues adopt Hedlyner as their operating system for live music.
2024–Present
Scaling the ecosystem
Now live in 50+ cities, Hedlyner connects thousands of artists to hundreds of venues. Enterprise agencies manage 40+ rooms on a single account. The mission is the same — the scope is bigger.
What we stand for

Our principles

🎵

Industry-first, always

We come from live music. Every feature is built by people who've run venues, toured stages, or negotiated contracts — not just engineers who've read about it.

⚡

Speed over bureaucracy

A booking should take minutes, not days. We obsess over removing every unnecessary step — from offer to deposit to show night — so venues and artists can focus on the music.

🤝

Fair for both sides

We don't play favorites. Hedlyner is built to give venues the tools to scale and artists the visibility to grow — because a healthy ecosystem requires both to win.

🔍

Radical transparency

No hidden fees. No surprise deductions. Artists see exactly what they'll earn. Venues see exactly what they'll pay. Every transaction is documented and traceable.

🌆

Built for community

Live music isn't just entertainment — it's the heartbeat of a neighborhood. We design Hedlyner to strengthen local scenes, not extract from them.

📈

Data-driven decisions

Gut feelings are great on stage. Off stage, you need numbers. Hedlyner surfaces the data talent buyers need to book smarter — which artists perform, which nights sell, which venues scale.
