The Problem

Booking talent is broken.
You already know it.

Agencies managing multiple venues are held together by spreadsheets, group chats, and hope. Every venue has a different system. Nothing talks to anything else.

Spreadsheet hell

One for each room. None of them current. Half your Monday is reconciling what actually got confirmed last week.

Email rabbit holes

Offers buried in inboxes. Contracts attached to threads from three months ago. Artists wondering if they're actually confirmed.

Payment chaos

Venmo, check, wire — whatever the artist prefers. 1099s at year end require archaeological digs through your records.

No visibility across rooms

Your team is booking in silos. You can't see across venues until something goes wrong — then you're fixing it live.

Artist discovery is manual

You rely on gut feel and personal networks. Finding new regional talent for specific rooms takes hours you don't have.

Reporting doesn't exist

Which nights drew? What did you spend on talent? Which rooms are profitable? You're guessing at best, flying blind at worst.
