Good to know

Payment processing

Credit card payments: 3.5% pass-through. ACH transfers: 1% pass-through. These are payment network fees — not Hedlyner revenue.

Artist placement

When Hedlyner sources and places an artist on your behalf (talent buying service), an 8% service fee applies to that booking only.

NIVA membership

Venue HQ and above qualify for NIVA member pricing. Contact us to verify your membership before subscribing.
